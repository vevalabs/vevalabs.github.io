<div class="content" style="max-width: 720px; font-size: 1.125rem; line-height: 1.75;">
VevaLabs is a research group led by <a>Doğan Ulus</a> dedicated to advancing system verification and validation (V&V) for autonomous and robotic systems. We focus on developing principled, scalable, and trustworthy methods to ensure the correctness, safety, and resilience of complex cyber-physical systems operating in real-world environments.
</div>

<div class="hx:mt-6 hx:mb-6"></div>

<div class="content" style="max-width: 720px; font-size: 1.125rem; line-height: 1.75;">
Our work bridges <a>#formal methods</a>, <a>#cyber-physical systems</a>, and <a>#cloud-native technologies</a> to enable automated and robust V&V pipelines. By combining mathematical rigor with modern infrastructure, we build tools and workflows that support continuous testing, system-level assurance, and scalable deployment across diverse platforms.
</div>
